Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VoiceSafe ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information when you use our application for recording and transcribing audio.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy will help you understand your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

2. Information We Collect

VoiceSafe is designed with a privacy-first approach:

Recordings are processed ONLY ON DEVICE. They NEVER leave your device. There is NO cloud processing, NO server transmission, and NO third-party sharing of your audio content.

Information we do NOT collect:

  • Personally identifiable information (name, email, address)
  • Location data or GPS coordinates on our servers
  • Access to your photo gallery or contacts
  • Device identifiers for ad tracking
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data

Locally Stored Information:

  • Your recordings and transcriptions
  • Bookmarks and summary notes
  • App settings

All recordings and processed data remain exclusively on your device and are never transmitted to any server or third party. Your private conversations stay private and NEVER LEAVE YOUR DEVICE.

3. On-Device Processing

All audio recordings are processed exclusively on your device using Apple's on-device Speech framework. Recordings are transcribed locally using Apple Intelligence, with NO data being sent to the cloud or any external server. Your private conversations stay private and never leave your device.

4. Third-Party Services

App Store:

The app download is processed through Apple's App Store. Apple may collect account information and purchase history in accordance with its own privacy policy. We do not have access to this data.

No other third-party services are integrated into this app.

5. How We Use Information

Since all data is stored locally on your device, we use it solely to:

  • Remember your preferred settings
  • Provide a seamless and personalized user experience
  • Keep your recordings organized and accessible

6. Data Security

Your recordings and data are securely stored on your device, leveraging built-in iOS security features:

  • Data is protected using native iOS encryption
  • Information is sandboxed within the app
  • No data transmission to external servers
  • Data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app

7. Your Privacy Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • You can delete your recordings and data directly within the app
  • You can uninstall the app to completely remove all data
  • You have full control over your personal information

8. Children's Privacy

VoiceSafe does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed for voice recording and transcription purposes and does not collect identifying information from any users.

9. International Users

VoiceSafe is available worldwide. Since all data remains on your device, no international data transfers take place.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app, and the "Last updated" date will be revised. Your continued use of the app implies acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your rights, please feel free to contact us:

info.foiasoft@gmail.com

We will respond to privacy-related inquiries within 30 days.

12. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Union, we operate in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since we do not collect personal data from servers, most GDPR rights (such as data access, correction, and deletion) apply primarily to your control of locally stored data.

13. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents are protected by the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Because VoiceSafe does not collect or sell any personal information to third parties, the requirements of this law are minimized for our app. All your data remains on your device and is not shared with advertisers or other third parties.
